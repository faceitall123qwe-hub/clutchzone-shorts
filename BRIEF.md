## Intent

Three vertical promo shorts for Clutchzone, Tim's free browser shooter (clutchzone.pro), to post on TikTok
(@whataspeed2) and YouTube Shorts. Tim: "zrób je ładnie" — the earlier shorts were plain cuts of real-time
screen recordings and stuttered badly, so smooth footage and a designed edit are the point of this run.
Bright, blocky, cartoon, game-like energy — the game's own look (sunny colourful town, chibi soldiers).

1. **Clutch** — "1 vs 5. Can I clutch it?" A kill counter fills up to 5/5 and lands on CLUTCH!
2. **Arsenal** — "6 weapons. 1 browser tab." Quick cuts, one kill per weapon, the weapon's name slapped on.
3. **Laptop** — "Your laptop can't run big shooters?" → "This one runs in a browser tab." Inspired by an
   itch.io player who loves it "especially for my bad laptop".

## Assets

- game-3d/marketing/raw/*-film.mp4 + .json — 60 fps gameplay filmed frame by frame (scripts/film.py), with
  the game's own sound; the json lists each kill's time and weapon slot.
- game-3d/marketing/fonts/LilitaOne-Regular.ttf — the game's display font (HUD, logo).

## Customizations

- Music: synthesized upbeat beat (HeyGen catalog needs sign-in; offline engine instead).
- SFX: whooshes and bass impacts from the bundled media-use library on cuts and kill stamps.
